GENE EXPRESSION (Protein Synthesis)

When RNA Helps Make Proteins Based On The DNA Instructions.
RNA stands for ribonucleic acid.
RNA is single stranded.
RNA is made of nucleotides, like DNA.
Base: Adenine (A), Cytosine [C], Guanine (G) *URACIL (u)

3 TYPES OF RNA

mRNA (messenger RNA) - Complimentary to DNA made in the nucleus - brings instructions to the ribosome.
rRNA (ribosomal RNA) - makes up ribosomes.
tRNA (Transfer RNA) - Brings amino acids (AA) to the ribosome to make proteins. Amino acids are the building blocks of proteins.

Gene expression occurs in 2 Steps

1. Transcription - Cell getting the DNA instructions, making mRNA with it. (Happens inside of nucleus because of using DNA)

DNA pattern example for mRNA: “A C G T T A G A”, While The Original Was “T G C A A T C T”


2. Translation - 3 types of RNA work together to link Amino acids together —> Make A Protein.
